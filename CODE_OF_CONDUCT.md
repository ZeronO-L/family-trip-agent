# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want Family Trip Agent to be a respectful, useful, and privacy-conscious open-source project. Participation should remain welcoming regardless of experience, identity, background, location, or preferred AI host.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas and evidence, not personal characteristics.
- Give specific, actionable, and respectful feedback.
- Accept that regional travel knowledge and tool access vary.
- Protect travelers' privacy, especially children's information and active-trip details.
- Disclose uncertainty and correct mistakes without hostility.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Publishing private information, booking details, live locations, or identifying travel data without permission.
- Deliberately misleading safety, legal, road, weather, or booking claims.
- Spam, impersonation, or coordinated disruption.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove content and may restrict participation when behavior threatens safety, privacy, or constructive collaboration. Public repository conduct can also be reported through GitHub's platform reporting tools. Do not include private travel information in a conduct report.

Enforcement decisions should be proportional, documented when practical, and focused on protecting contributors and users.
